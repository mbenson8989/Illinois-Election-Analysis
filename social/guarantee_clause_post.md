## Citation check (done November 24, 2026, before drafting)

- Federal Register API, https://www.federalregister.gov/api/v1/documents/2026-06601.json: executive order number 14399, "Ensuring Citizenship Verification and Integrity in Federal Elections," signing date 2026-03-31, publication date 2026-04-03, citation **91 FR 17125**.
- Official GPO PDF, https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-04-03/pdf/2026-06601.pdf (sha256 8648c1a925357a874d49aa32952a925467400282e500e4c192ebf4ddcd8d85d3). Verbatim on p. 17125:
  - Header: "Executive Order 14399 of March 31, 2026"
  - Authority clause: "... the Federal Government's constitutional obligation to guarantee a republican form of Government to every State in the Union, U.S. Const. Art. IV, Sec. 4, it is hereby ordered:"
  - Sec. 1: "The Federal Government has an unavoidable duty under Article II of the Constitution of the United States to enforce Federal law, which includes preventing violations of Federal criminal law and maintaining public confidence in election outcomes."
- U.S. Constitution, Art. IV, Sec. 4 (National Archives transcript): "The United States shall guarantee to every State in this Union a Republican Form of Government, and shall protect each of them against Invasion; ..."

**Accuracy note:** in the order, "unavoidable duty" is tied to **Article II**, not to Article IV. The post says the order *cites* the Guarantee Clause and *separately* says the federal government has an "unavoidable duty" to enforce federal law. It does not claim the Guarantee Clause itself uses the word "unavoidable." The graphic's quotations are verbatim, with ellipses only where words are left out.
